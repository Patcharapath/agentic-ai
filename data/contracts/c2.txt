CLOUD DATA SERVICES AGREEMENT
Agreement No. CDS-2026-118

This Agreement is made on 3 November 2026 between DataWork Co., Ltd. (the "Customer") and SkyStore Pte. Ltd., Singapore (the "Provider").

1. Services. The Provider will host the Customer's customer-relationship database, which contains the names, phone numbers and purchase histories of the Customer's clients, for 12 months.
2. Fees. The fee is 18,000 THB per month (216,000 THB for the term).
3. Service levels. The Provider guarantees 99.5% monthly availability and will respond to any reported incident within 2 hours.
4. Payment. Invoices are payable within 15 days of the invoice date.
5. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice.
6. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
