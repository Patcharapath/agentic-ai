OFFICE CLEANING SERVICES AGREEMENT
Agreement No. OCS-2026-022

This Agreement is made on 7 November 2026 between DataWork Co., Ltd. (the "Client") and Sparkle Facility Services Co., Ltd. (the "Contractor").

1. Services. The Contractor will clean the Client's Bangkok office every working day, including floors, kitchens and restrooms, for 12 months.
2. Fees. The monthly fee is 25,000 THB, a total of 300,000 THB for the term.
3. Staff. The Contractor will provide two trained cleaners and will replace any absent cleaner on the same day.
4. Payment. The Client will pay each monthly invoice within 30 days of receiving it.
5. Termination. Either party may end this Agreement with 30 days' written notice.
6. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Thailand.

Signed for the Client: Thanakorn Srisuk, Office Manager
Signed for the Contractor: Ratana Wongsa, Managing Director
